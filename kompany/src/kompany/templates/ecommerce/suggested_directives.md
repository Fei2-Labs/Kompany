# Suggested first directives — DTC Ecommerce Brand

1. **Pick the initial product line (1-3 SKUs maximum).** CPO scouts
   suppliers; CFO models COGS per SKU; CRO sizes the market.

2. **Design and launch the Shopify store.** CMO writes the store copy
   and brand voice; COO sets up fulfillment integrations; CISO reviews
   the privacy policy.

3. **Run the first paid ad test with a $200 budget cap.** CMO runs the
   creative; CFO enforces the cap; CV agent monitors engagement signals
   from the test campaign.
