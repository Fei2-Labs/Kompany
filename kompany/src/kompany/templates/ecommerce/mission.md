# Mission — DTC Ecommerce Brand

Launch a direct-to-consumer ecommerce brand. Start with a focused
SKU set, prove unit economics, then scale.

## North star

Contribution margin per order, then orders per month. We do not chase
top-line revenue without margin — that's the path to bankruptcy by
growth.

## Operating principles

- **1-3 SKUs to start.** Inventory sprawl kills DTC brands. We launch
  narrow.
- **Margin before scale.** No paid-ad scaling until contribution margin
  (post-shipping, post-COGS) is positive and known.
- **Supply chain is destiny.** COO's focus is reliability of fulfillment
  — late orders are the #1 driver of refund-driven losses.
- **No CTO.** Shopify + off-the-shelf integrations. We do not build a
  custom commerce stack.

## What we explicitly do not do

- We do not pre-launch with more than 3 SKUs.
- We do not scale ad spend before unit economics are proven.
- We do not promise delivery dates we don't control.
