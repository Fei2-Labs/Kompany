# Mission — Blank Slate

(set your mission with `kompany config set mission ...`)

This template is for advanced players who want to start from a
completely empty canvas. All 11 C-suite agents are enabled and you
have $1,000 in starting capital, but there is no preset mission and
no suggested directives.

Use `kompany directive "..."` to send your first directive when you
know what you want to build.
