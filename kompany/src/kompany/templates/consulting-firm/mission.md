# Mission — Consulting Firm

Run a consulting firm where the **majority of revenue is recurring
retainers**, not one-off engagements.

## North star

Monthly retainer revenue. One-off project revenue is fine for cash
flow, but the long-term goal is predictable recurring income from a
small number of high-trust clients.

## Operating principles

- **Three tiers, not bespoke.** We sell pre-packaged service tiers.
  Custom proposals are an exception we charge a premium for.
- **Senior delivery only.** No CTO or CPO on staff — we don't build
  products; we advise on them. Engagements are senior-led.
- **Cash is king.** CFO threshold is $1,000 because consulting margins
  are good and we'd rather move on opportunities than micromanage
  spend.
- **Trust is the moat.** The CSA (chief security advisor) and CISO are
  on the team because client trust is what justifies the price.

## What we explicitly do not do

- We do not build SaaS products on the side.
- We do not take engagements we can't staff senior.
- We do not chase logos at the expense of margin.
