# Suggested first directives — Consulting Firm

1. **Define our three service tiers and their deliverables.** CRO and
   COO co-author the tier sheet; CFO prices each tier against a target
   gross margin.

2. **Land our first 3 paying retainer clients.** CMO writes the
   outreach sequence; CRO runs the calls; CoS tracks the pipeline.

3. **Set up monthly retainer billing and a client onboarding doc.**
   CFO picks the billing tool; COO writes the onboarding doc; CISO
   reviews data-handling clauses.
