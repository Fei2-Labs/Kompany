# Suggested first directives — Indie Tool

1. **Pick one narrow pain we can solve in 2 weeks.** CPO and CTO
   shortlist three candidates; CEO picks one and writes a one-page
   product brief.

2. **Ship the MVP and put it behind a Gumroad paywall.** CTO builds;
   CFO sets the price; COO handles the Gumroad listing.

3. **Launch on Product Hunt with a clear demo video.** CRO writes the
   launch post; CPO records the demo; CoS schedules the launch day.
