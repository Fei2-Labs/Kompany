# Mission — Indie Tool

Ship a paid indie tool and grow it to **$10k MRR** within 12 months.

## North star

Monthly recurring revenue from a single, narrow, paid product.
$10k/mo is enough to quit a day job; everything else is noise.

## Operating principles

- **Pick one pain.** No platforms, no suites — one tool that does one
  thing visibly better than the alternatives.
- **Ship in two-week sprints.** If a feature can't land in a sprint,
  it's too big — split it or cut it.
- **Solo-founder mode.** The team is small on purpose: CEO, CFO, CTO,
  CPO, CRO, COO, CoS. No CMO (you do your own marketing), no CSA, no
  CISO (until there's revenue worth securing).
- **Cheap by default.** CFO threshold is $100; anything bigger needs
  explicit approval.

## What we explicitly do not do

- We do not raise outside capital.
- We do not hire employees in year one.
- We do not build features customers haven't asked for.
