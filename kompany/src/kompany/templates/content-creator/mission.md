# Mission — Content Creator Studio

Build a media studio: one primary channel + one paid newsletter.
Goal: a sustainable creator business, not a viral lottery ticket.

## North star

Paid newsletter subscribers. Channel reach is a top-of-funnel input;
the business runs on the newsletter's recurring revenue.

## Operating principles

- **Cadence over virality.** A publish-every-week post that's a 7/10
  beats a once-a-quarter 10/10. The CMO is hired to enforce cadence.
- **One format, mastered.** Pick YouTube, podcast, or written — don't
  fan out until one channel is profitable.
- **The CV agent matters.** We track Creator/Viewer engagement metrics
  (the `cv` role) because they're the leading indicator of newsletter
  conversion.
- **No CTO, no CISO.** We use off-the-shelf tools (Substack, YouTube,
  ConvertKit). We don't build infra.

## What we explicitly do not do

- We do not take sponsorships that compromise editorial trust.
- We do not switch formats every quarter.
- We do not optimize for short-term metrics that don't compound.
