# Suggested first directives — Content Creator Studio

1. **Pick the primary channel format (YouTube, podcast, or written).**
   CMO presents the trade-offs; CRO models revenue per channel; CEO
   picks.

2. **Schedule and publish the first 4 posts on a fixed cadence.** CoS
   builds the editorial calendar; CMO writes; CV agent reviews each
   post against engagement hypotheses.

3. **Launch a paid newsletter with a free + paid tier.** CPO picks the
   newsletter platform; CRO writes the upsell copy; CFO sets the
   pricing.
