# Mission — SaaS Startup

Build a B2B SaaS product and grow it to **$1M ARR** within 18 months.

## North star

Annual recurring revenue. Every project, every directive, every dollar
spent should map back to "does this move ARR?"

## Operating principles

- **ICP first.** We sell to a narrow, well-understood customer. No
  spray-and-pray marketing.
- **Self-serve over enterprise.** Default to credit-card checkout and
  product-led growth. Sales-led motions are exceptions, not the rule.
- **Ship weekly.** Cadence beats perfection. The CFO's $500 approval
  threshold is high enough that the CTO can move without daily friction.
- **AI cost is a real cost.** Every LLM call hits the ledger. The CFO
  reviews AI spend weekly.

## What we explicitly do not do

- We do not build hardware.
- We do not run paid ads until we have at least 10 paying customers
  acquired organically — paid acquisition without a known LTV is
  gambling.
- We do not raise capital in year one.
