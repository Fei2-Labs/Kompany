# Suggested first directives — SaaS Startup

Pick one to send to the CEO with `kompany directive "..."`. Each
appears as a draft project in your inbox; approve the one you want to
work on first.

1. **Define our ideal customer profile and pricing tiers.** The CRO and
   CPO collaborate to write the ICP doc; the CFO validates pricing
   against unit economics.

2. **Launch a landing page with a waitlist signup.** The CTO ships the
   page; the CMO writes the copy; the CRO sets up the lead-capture
   funnel and tags the source.

3. **Set up Stripe with a 14-day free trial and self-serve checkout.**
   The CTO does the integration; the CFO reviews fee structure and
   refund policy; the CISO signs off on PCI handling.
